ALGORITHM: Choose the cheapest mobile recharge plan

Plans (price, validity, data per day):
  Plan A: Rs 149, 20 days, 1 GB per day
  Plan B: Rs 239, 28 days, 1.5 GB per day
  Plan C: Rs 349, 56 days, 3 GB per day

A plan "fits" when its data per day is at least the daily need AND its validity is at least the days needed.

1. START.
2. DEFINE the three plans A, B and C with their price, validity and data per day.
3. INPUT the daily data need in GB.
4. INPUT the number of days needed.
5. SET best plan to NONE and best price to 99999.
6. TAKE the next plan from the list (A, then B, then C).
7. IF the plan fits the need, GO TO step 8, ELSE GO TO step 10.
8. IF the plan price is less than the best price, GO TO step 9, ELSE GO TO step 10.
9. SET best plan to this plan and best price to this plan's price.
10. IF more plans remain in the list, GO TO step 6, ELSE GO TO step 11.
11. IF best plan is NONE, GO TO step 12, ELSE GO TO step 13.
12. PRINT "No plan fits your need" and GO TO step 14.
13. PRINT the best plan name and its price.
14. END.
